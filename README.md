# PARCEL OS

## A collaborative WebOS!

This is ParcelOS, a nice little project I've been working on for Crossroads! Just open it in your browser (https://richardkiselev.github.io/PassTheParcel). I was inspired to do this after seeing how great the Hack Club community was. I really just wanted to make a cool collaborative project that everyone could participate in. It would also make putting in hours easier. Instead of coming up with new complex systems, I could just make apps and add features.

I took some inspiration from the Hack Club webOS Jam series, but I generally took my own direction after setting up the canvas and the apps.

Here are the neat features:

- App icons: click them to open draggable windows
- Random background upon launch, can be changed in an app. Three different options.
- Apps for information, how to add apps and a list of collaborators.
- Cat clicker game! Click the cat, and it cycles through images. Buttons to buy more CPS and %PS.
- Malware! When you click it, it opens 20 windows of a silly pop-up in an attempt to emulate old windowspam malware.
- Time and date in the top bar! Also, spinning capybaras.
- Easy modular additions. Anyone can add apps just by sending over an index.html. That's why it's a COLLABORATIVE WebOS. Hopefully, I'll get some peeps sending in apps that I can add.
